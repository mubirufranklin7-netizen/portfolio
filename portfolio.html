About Me

I am an Electrical Engineering student at Kyambogo University, Uganda, currently in my third year. I am interested in electrical systems, industrial automation, electronics, embedded systems, control systems, renewable energy, and applying software and artificial intelligence to engineering problems.

Through classroom projects, laboratory work, programming exercises and technical research, I have learned how engineering concepts become practical systems that solve real-world problems.

My long-term goal is to design, implement, maintain and improve modern electrical and automated systems.
